# FloodWatch Research Programme

**Research baseline:** v1.0 — 16 September 2026

This directory records the research programme supporting FloodWatch Nigeria. The software/hardware system is treated as an experimental testbed; the repository does **not** claim that combining IoT, machine learning, dashboards, GIS, or alerts is itself novel.

## Frozen central question

> Under the evaluated flood-monitoring conditions, does additional predictive and sensing complexity provide a materially useful early-warning advantage over simpler interpretable warning strategies?

## Evidence layers

1. **Independent hydrological observations** — predictive validity and warning performance.
2. **Controlled simulation** — scenario, integration, edge-case and fault testing.
3. **Physical prototype** — implementation feasibility of the sensing-to-decision pipeline.

Simulation must not be represented as evidence of real Nigerian predictive accuracy, false-alarm performance, lead time, or generalisation.
