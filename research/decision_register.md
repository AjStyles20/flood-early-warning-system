# Research Decision Register

## DEC-001 — Broad architecture is not the novelty
Do not claim IoT + ML + dashboard/GIS + alerts as the novel contribution. Prior-art collision is substantial.

## DEC-002 — Simulator evidence is limited
Simulator-generated telemetry may support development, integration and controlled scenario/fault testing but cannot establish real-world Nigerian predictive accuracy, false alarms, lead time or generalisation.

## DEC-003 — Independent hydrological evidence required
The decisive predictive conclusions require observational or clearly identified reanalysis hydrological data.

## DEC-004 — Strong baseline before ML verdict
B3 must be a calibrated, credible conventional anticipatory warning method and must not be deliberately weakened.

## DEC-005 — No random row split for decisive evaluation
Temporal/event independence is required because hydrological time series are autocorrelated.

## DEC-006 — Model is evidence-selected
Random Forest is a candidate treatment, not a predetermined winner. Logistic Regression and strong conventional methods remain legitimate outcomes.

## DEC-007 — Cross-basin testing is validation
Spatial transfer/generalisation testing may strengthen evidence but is not claimed as the core novelty.

## DEC-008 — Final title and contribution remain provisional
They will be frozen after data qualification and decisive evidence, not before.

## DEC-009 — Preserve negative results
Failed models, unhelpful sensors, horizon collapse, acquisition limitations and baseline dominance are research evidence and must be logged rather than hidden.

## DEC-010 — Supervisor gate before expensive experimental expansion
Present the narrowed proposal and experimental contract to the supervisor early. Record approval, modifications, conditions or rejection and update the contract accordingly.
